Psychosis-related disorganisation impairs the construction and verbal expression of relational cognitive maps from visual experience

Xiaoyan Wu, Peizheng Wu, Finn Rabe, Wolfgang Omlor, Victoria Edkins, Yves Pauli, Stephanie Theves, Wolfram Hinzen, Iris E. Sommer, Philipp Homan

Paper 1 · cognitive maps

Disorganization impairs cognitive maps built from visual inputs

Cognitive disorganization selectively reduces spatial sensitivity and spatial generalization when a relational map has to be built from visual input, but not when the same structure is given through language.

Read the paper
Paper 2 · verbal expression

Teaching others externalizes spatial structure, selectively after visual learning

Explaining what was learned to another person brings two-dimensional structure into speech, and higher disorganization shows up as answers that stray from the question asked.
